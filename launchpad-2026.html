Why this exists

Nobody hands you a flight plan for after graduation.

Lecture halls teach the theory. They rarely cover how recruitment actually works, what employers screen for, or how to turn a business idea into a business. LAUNCHPAD closes that gap in one focused day.

01
Limited exposure to the world of work before students ever sit an interview.
02
A widening gap between academic knowledge and the employability skills recruiters screen for.
03
Little visibility into career paths beyond the obvious — development, consulting, finance, enterprise.
04
Real uncertainty about what comes next, right when final-year students need clarity most.
